    Prayer guide

    New to praying this way? You're very welcome here. There are no special words you need to know. Speak to God simply and from your heart, the way you would talk to someone who loves you. You can pray aloud, pray quietly, or simply read a scripture over Naomi. Every prayer counts.

    What to pray for

    • The complete and full restoration of Naomi's heart, with strength in every beat
    • Strength and peace over her mind and body
    • Rest, good sleep, and strength for her daily life
    • Wisdom and favor with everyone caring for her
    • Focus, clear understanding, and energy for her PharmD studies, and favor with her professors and classmates
    • Grace to keep up with her classes, research, and deadlines, with peace instead of pressure
    • Strength, unity, and faith for the whole family

    Scriptures to pray

    Feel free to use your own scriptures.

    • Jeremiah 30:17
      “For I will restore health unto thee, and I will heal thee of thy wounds, saith the LORD.”
    • Psalm 103:2–3
      “Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits: Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases.”
    • Isaiah 53:5
      “…and with his stripes we are healed.”
    • Ezekiel 36:26
      “A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you.”
    • Isaiah 41:10
      “Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee.”
    • James 5:15
      “And the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up.”

    During your hour

    1. Join the Zoom call a few minutes early, and stay muted unless you're praying aloud.
    2. Begin with praise and thanksgiving.
    3. Read one or two scriptures aloud.
    4. Pray through the prayer points, naming Naomi's heart specifically.
    5. If you're leading, invite each person to pray in turn, and stay on until the next hour's leader arrives.
